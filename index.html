Happy Birthday, Yaya

Today, the 20th of August, is your birthday.

Even though we can't be together now, I still yearn for your happiness. I wish for you to always be healthy. I wish that everything in your life becomes better, brighter, and kinder to you.

And when things become heavy, I want you to know that you are not alone. Please, never give up. Never lose your hope.

Even if there are days when everything feels too much, I hope you can find a reason to keep going.

I still see your silhouette every time I look up at the moon, because somehow, the moon is the only thing that comes close to reminding me of your beauty.

Thank you for existing in the same

timeline.
universe.
world.

So once again...

Happy Birthday, Yaya.
